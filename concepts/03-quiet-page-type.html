Concept 3 · Blush (Van’s preference)

Quiet Page

A calm, book-like page. Text is left-aligned, headings sit in a left column with reading text on the right, and there’s plenty of blush space. It feels less “wedding stationery” and more personal letter.

Typography decisions

  • Literata replaces Cormorant. Its larger x-height and sturdier serifs match Noto Serif TC’s optical size, so mixed lines need no size-adjust (100%).
  • The heading weight drops to 500 in both scripts. The two serifs then have similar stroke contrast and colour on the page.
  • This is the most robust option for long Chinese paragraphs. The left-aligned, 34-character measure keeps rhythm without justification gaps.
RoleFontLoading
English headingsLiterata 500literata latin + latin-ext, 1 file each, preloaded (latin)
English body, nav, forms, buttonsSource Sans 3 400 / 6002 weights × latin/latin-ext
Chinese headingsNoto Serif TC 500 / SC 500unicode-range slices, CJK weight 1 of 2
Chinese body, nav, forms, buttonsNoto Sans TC 400 / SC 400unicode-range slices, CJK weight 2 of 2

Every page sets font-synthesis: none, font-display: swap and Chinese letter-spacing: normal. Latin is first in every stack, then the CJK web font, then system CJK fonts. On Chinese pages the Latin display face is re-declared with size-adjust: 100%. Chinese uses one body weight, so emphasis uses colour, not bold.
