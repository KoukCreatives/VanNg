Van Ng website: eight bilingual concepts

Each concept is a working home page in English and Traditional Chinese, with a Simplified test page and a type-system sheet. The image boxes are placeholders for Van’s authorised photos. Concepts 1 and 2 are the fair palette pair: same layout, type, crop and wording, with only the colour direction changed. Start with those when asking Van which feels warm, trustworthy and genuinely her.

Concept 4

Soft Sans

Rose & Ivory (gold-free fallback)

All sans serif, gold-free and gentle. Rounded panels, dusty-rose and sage shapes, and an easy mobile rhythm. It’s the most practical, modern and legible direction, and the lightest to load.

English
Source Sans 3 600 + Source Sans 3 400 / 600
中文
Noto Sans TC 500 + Noto Sans TC 400
Gold
None (Rose & Ivory fallback). Dusty rose and sage are decorative blobs only (2.35:1 and 2.88:1). They never carry text.

Decisions needed before Round 2

  • Traditional, Simplified or both. The Traditional pages are designed first because of the poster and the testimonial screenshot, but that is a clue, not a decision. Every concept already swaps to Noto SC.
  • Blush or lucky red, and whether any decorative gold feels genuine to Van. Concepts 3, 4, 6, 7 and 8 work with no gold at all.
  • Glossary terms for the fluent reviewer: 證婚人, 粵語 / 廣東話, 普通話 / 國語, 民事結合, 索取報價.
  • Whether the LXGW WenKai accent (Concept 5) or the vertical phrase (Concept 8) is welcome. Both are optional and decorative.
  • Final URLs (/ceremonies/ and the Chinese URL pattern). The concepts use flat file names only so they can be browsed from GitHub.
