A ceremony with your story at its heart

The words you share can be one of the most moving parts of your wedding. What matters is that they express your own feelings, rather than someone else's idea of what you should say.

In my celebrant work, heartfelt blessings matter to me alongside the responsibilities of the role. I want the ceremony to celebrate the two of you, not draw attention away from you.

Your ceremony is a chance to put your story into words, with the people who matter to you beside you. I hope those words feel personal when you say them, and meaningful when you remember them.

In that moment, I was reminded just how powerful words can be.

Van, reflecting on a ceremony she led

Questions before enquiring

Which languages do you offer?

I offer personalised wedding ceremonies in English, Cantonese and Mandarin. For a ceremony in more than one language, tell me what you have in mind and we can talk it through.

Where do you hold ceremonies?

East, central and west Auckland, the North Shore, Rangitoto Island, Hamilton and the South Island. Travel costs apply outside Auckland.

How much does a ceremony cost?

Every ceremony is different, so I’ll put together a quote for yours once I know your date, location and plans.

Do you offer civil union ceremonies?

Yes. I’m registered as a marriage and civil union celebrant, and civil union ceremonies are personalised in the same way.

Does an enquiry hold our date?

No. Sending an enquiry doesn’t reserve your date. Availability and booking arrangements are confirmed separately.

Does a ceremony include legal advice?

No. When I’m your celebrant, I’m there as your celebrant only, so a ceremony booking doesn’t include legal advice.

Start with your date and your ideas

You do not need to have every detail settled before asking a question. A preferred date, ceremony location if known, and the languages you have in mind are useful starting points.

  • Your preferred date, or a few dates you are considering
  • Where you plan to marry, if you know
  • The languages you, your families and guests speak
